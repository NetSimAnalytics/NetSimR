# Articles

### Articles

- [From pricing to capital and reserves: a motor
  portfolio](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/EndToEnd.md):

  A motor portfolio through a non-life actuary’s work in R: GLM pricing,
  claims simulation, excess of loss reinsurance, capital, reserves, IFRS
  17 and Solvency II.

- [Rate reviews: on-level premiums, the indicated rate change and
  credibility](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/PricingMonitoring.md):

  A rate review with the loss ratio method in R: on-level premiums by
  the parallelogram method, trended losses, the indicated rate change
  and credibility.

- [Simulating claims with
  simulate_claims()](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/SimulateClaims.md):

  Simulating insurance claims in R: frequency and severity
  distributions, Pareto tails, caps, and excess of loss layers with
  limited reinstatements.

- [Claims reserving with the chain ladder and its
  relatives](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/Reserving.md):

  Claims reserving in R: the chain ladder with Mack’s standard errors,
  Bornhuetter-Ferguson, Cape Cod, the bootstrap, the one-year view and
  the Munich chain ladder.

- [Pricing an excess of loss
  layer](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/ReinsurancePricing.md):

  Pricing an excess of loss layer in R: burning cost, Pareto rating,
  exposure rating with a risk profile, credibility and the technical
  premium.

- [Capped means, exposure curves and increased limit
  factors](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/CappedMean.md):

  Capped means, exposure curves and increased limit factors for
  LogNormal, Gamma, Pareto and sliced severities, with the theory and
  worked examples in R.

- [Sliced LogNormal-Pareto and Gamma-Pareto
  distributions](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/SlicedDistributions.md):

  Sliced LogNormal-Pareto and Gamma-Pareto distributions for claim
  severities with a heavy tail: densities, distribution functions,
  quantiles and means in R.

- [Pure IBNR exposure from a reporting delay
  distribution](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/PureIBNR.md):

  Pure IBNR claim counts and unearned exposure from a LogNormal or Gamma
  reporting delay distribution, with the theory and worked examples in
  R.
