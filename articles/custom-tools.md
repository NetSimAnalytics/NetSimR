# Custom tools for insurers and reinsurers

NetSimR and its six tools are free. The same approach — actuarial
methods validated against established packages, in tools that people who
do not write R can use — can be built around your own data, assumptions
and processes.

## What can be built

### Pricing

GLM rating models, rate tables ready for a rating engine, scoring of new
business, and rate reviews with on-levelling, trend and credibility.

### Reserving, IFRS 17 and Solvency II

Reserving tools on your triangles, with discounting, the risk
adjustment, the standard formula's reserve risk, and reports for the
reserving committee.

### Reinsurance

Experience and exposure rating of treaties, and the comparison of
programmes by their cost and the capital they save.

### Capital

Simulation of the portfolio's lines with their dependence, capital by
line, and the comparison with the standard formula.

## How the work is done

- **Your data, where it lives**: databases, Excel workbooks or files,
  read as they are.
- **Your data stays yours**: a tool runs on your own servers or on each
  actuary’s computer, and sends nothing outside, as [NetSimR’s
  tools](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/try-online.html#your-data-stays-on-your-computer)
  do.
- **Your methods and assumptions**: the tool does what your actuaries do
  today, faster and the same way every time, with the choices they make
  in it recorded.
- **Validated**: results checked against established packages and hand
  calculations, with automated tests, as NetSimR’s are.
- **Documented**: reports that explain the figures to a committee, an
  auditor or a regulator.
- **Delivered the way you work**: a tool in the browser, on your own
  servers or on each actuary’s computer, or an R package for your team,
  with training and a handover.

## See the tools

pricing to capital and
reserves](https://netsimanalytics.github.io/NetSimR/articles/EndToEnd.md)
follows one portfolio through the same steps in code.
