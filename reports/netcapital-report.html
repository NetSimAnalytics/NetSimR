How to read this report

  • The VaR at 99.5% is the loss exceeded in 0.5% of the years; the TVaR is the mean of those years.
  • The diversification benefit is how much less capital the lines need together than on their own.
  • Each line keeps its own distribution: the copula only orders the lines' losses, so that their ranks have its dependence.
  • The figures are those of the simulations: with more simulations, the tail figures move less from one run to another.
