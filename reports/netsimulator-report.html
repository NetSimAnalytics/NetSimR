How to read this report

  • A large gap between the median and the tail percentiles points to a heavy-tailed outcome.
  • VaR is the loss exceeded only with the stated probability. TVaR is the average loss in those worst cases, so it is always at least as large as VaR: TVaR 99.5% is the mean of the worst 0.5% of the simulations, rounded up to a whole number (here 100).
  • The return period shows the same probability as a frequency: 99.5% corresponds to a 1 in 200 year event.
  • Gross is before reinsurance. Ceded is what the layers pay, and net is what remains.
  • The accuracy ranges reflect simulation noise only, not uncertainty in the chosen distributions or parameters.
  • Figures reflect the tail adjustments and reinsurance structures listed under Model settings.
